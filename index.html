Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about loans?

Read these answers before contacting SICUL.

How much can I borrow?

The amount available depends on SICUL's actual products, eligibility rules, affordability assessment and credit approval. The calculator's upper limit is not a lending promise.

Can I calculate thousands of rands?

Yes. The calculator accepts amounts from R50 to R1,000,000 for illustrative calculations.

Does the calculator guarantee approval?

No. It provides a mathematical estimate only. It is not a loan offer, approval or credit agreement.

Why does the calculator use 30%?

Thirty percent is the example requested for this website. SICUL must confirm its approved pricing, fees and repayment terms before the calculator is used for actual loan quotations.

How do I submit the form?

Download the PDF and follow the official SICUL submission instructions. Contact SICUL if you need assistance.

How can I contact SICUL?

You can message either WhatsApp number or send an email using the contact details below.
