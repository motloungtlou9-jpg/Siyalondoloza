Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about loans?

Here are a few things to know before making an enquiry.

How much can I borrow?

The amount available depends on SIUCL's actual products, eligibility rules, affordability assessment and credit approval.

Can I calculate thousands of rands?

Yes. The calculator accepts amounts from R50 up to R1,000,000. This is a calculator limit, not a promise that SIUCL lends those amounts.

Does the calculator guarantee approval?

No. The calculation is an estimate only and is not a loan offer, approval or credit agreement.

Why does the calculator use 30%?

Thirty percent is based on the example requested for this website. SIUCL must confirm the actual rate, fee structure and repayment terms before the calculator is used to quote real loans.

How do I submit the form?

Download the PDF and follow the official SIUCL submission instructions. Contact SIUCL if you need assistance or clarification.
